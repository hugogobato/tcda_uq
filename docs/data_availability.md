# Data and result availability

The repository publishes simulation results, frozen experiment code, derived CT-pool summaries, manuscript figures, and reviewed aggregate clinical mean results. [The paper results guide](paper_results.md) maps these artifacts to the paper and distinguishes final production runs from earlier diagnostics.

## Simulations and image-pool benchmarks

All 11,200 final fitted-sample replication records are retained, together with 1,400 mean-stage replay sidecars from the same simulated PACCE samples. The PACCE-matched simulations are generated data, distinct from patient records from the trial. They include synthetic potential-outcome pairs for evaluating individual-effect coverage.

The SARS-CoV-2 benchmark uses a constructed image-pool contrast, with independent synthetic covariates and treatment assignment. The exact derived pools are under `reproduction/results/empirical_pool_inputs/`; original CT images are not redistributed. See the [versioned dataset paper](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2020.04.24.20078584v2) and its access information for the images. The available benchmark copy contains 1,252 COVID-19 and 1,229 non-COVID-19 images, whereas the dataset paper reports 1,230 in the latter group.

ORBIT outcomes are generated by the linked-twist-map construction described in the paper; no external patient data are involved.

## Clinical PACCE analysis

The PACCE trial (Amgen study 20040249) inputs require authorized access through [Project Data Sphere](https://data.projectdatasphere.org/projectdatasphere/html/content/84e64633-4d24-44cd-b9d6-67125010d4c2). The repository does not redistribute SAS data, subject-level preprocessing records, patient identifiers, individual topological trajectories, or clinical ITTE prediction curves. The manuscript reports aggregate summaries; it does not release those underlying individual objects.

Published clinical files are selected aggregate mean curves, mean confidence summaries, tests, support counts, and sensitivity diagnostics. Sanitized provenance removes identifier lists and local input paths. The numerical analysis remains exploratory: post-treatment observation selection and working regression models limit its interpretation to the targets stated in the paper. A confidence band containing zero does not establish equivalence or validate an unobserved counterfactual distribution.

Authorized investigators can reproduce the clinical calculations using the retained source versions and their own access-controlled inputs. Any files produced by those clinical scripts must be reviewed separately before publication; the full local output directory includes restricted objects and is not the public release allowlist.
