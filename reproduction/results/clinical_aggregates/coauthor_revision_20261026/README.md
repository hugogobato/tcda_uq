# Corrected PACCE aggregate diagnostics, 26 October 2026

`ctate_profile_summary.csv` is the corrected six-profile conditional-mean diagnostic table. Age is 61 years, matching the years unit in the data adapter. The historical `main_cohort/ctate_profile_summary.csv`, `ctate_curves.csv`, and `ctate_profiles.yaml` used age 6.1 and are retained solely as superseded provenance. The fitted population-average result does not query these profiles and is unchanged.

`independent_fold_sensitivity.csv` reports only aggregate fitted-sample diagnostics: the original treatment-stratified fit followed by five independently randomized two-fold fits. Every mean band includes zero throughout. This sensitivity does not certify nuisance rates, remove pooled-representation dependence, or identify a causal effect after post-treatment observation selection.

No patient records, subject identifiers, individual topological outcomes, clinical ITTE prediction curves, per-unit scores, or train/calibration membership arrays are published. Prediction-width summaries can be regenerated by authorized users outside the checkout; no clinical prediction-output file is included in this public correction directory.

The corrected portable entry point is `reproduction/pacce_coauthor_revision.py`, documented in `docs/pacce_coauthor_revision.md`. It requires separately authorized clinical inputs and writes only aggregate outputs outside the repository. Historical records and frozen sources remain unchanged.
